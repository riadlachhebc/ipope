📖 Guides

Setup Guides by Device

Step-by-step instructions for every major device. Most setups take under 5 minutes.

Amazon Firestick Setup

  1. Enable "Apps from Unknown Sources" in Settings
  2. Download Downloader app from Amazon Store
  3. Install IPTV Smarters Pro via Downloader
  4. Enter your M3U URL and credentials
  5. Start watching!

Smart TV Setup (Samsung/LG)

  1. Open your TV's App Store
  2. Search for "Smart IPTV" or "SS IPTV"
  3. Install the app (free)
  4. Upload your M3U URL via the app's web portal
  5. Restart the app and enjoy

PC / Mac with VLC

  1. Download VLC Media Player (free)
  2. Open VLC → Media → Open Network Stream
  3. Paste your M3U URL
  4. Click Play
  5. Browse channels in the Playlist view
🔧 Troubleshooting

Common Issues & Fixes

Self-solve most problems in minutes with these quick fixes.

1

Buffering or freezing streams

Check your internet speed (min. 10 Mbps for HD, 25 Mbps for 4K). Restart your router. Try switching to a wired connection. If the issue persists, contact support and we'll switch you to a different server.

2

Cannot connect / wrong credentials

Double-check username and password from your welcome email (copy-paste, don't type manually). Ensure your subscription is active. Contact support with your order email to verify.

3

Channel not loading or black screen

Try refreshing the channel list in your IPTV app. Restart the app completely. If one specific channel is down, it may be a temporary broadcast issue — try an alternative stream for the same channel.

4

EPG / programme guide not showing

In your IPTV app settings, check that the EPG URL is populated. Trigger a manual EPG update/refresh. Allow 5–10 minutes for the guide to load completely.

5

App crashes or won't open

Clear the app's cache and data in your device settings. Uninstall and reinstall the IPTV app. Ensure your device firmware/OS is up to date. Contact support if the issue continues.
