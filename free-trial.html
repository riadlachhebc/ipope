Supported Devices & Setup Guide

Detailed setup instructions for every major device. Our support team is also available 24/7.

Amazon Firestick

Install IPTV Smarters Pro or TiviMate from the Amazon App Store. Enter your M3U URL and credentials. Done in 3 minutes.

Samsung / LG Smart TV

Install Smart IPTV or SS IPTV from your TV's app store. Enter your M3U playlist URL or upload via the web portal.

Android TV Box

Download IPTV Smarters Pro or Tivimate directly from Google Play. Add your M3U URL and start streaming immediately.

iPhone / Android Phone

iOS: Use GSE Smart IPTV or IPTV Smarters. Android: IPTV Smarters Pro from Google Play. Add M3U URL and enjoy.

Windows / Mac

Use VLC Media Player (free) — open your M3U URL via Media > Open Network Stream. Also works with Kodi.

MAG Device

Enter your Xtream Codes or M3U URL in your MAG portal settings. Our support team will walk you through it step by step.
